01 / Purpose

A clear pathway towards employment.

Career Compass supported newly arrived migrants from ten nationalities to prepare for employment in aged care, healthcare and childcare. The program brought together employment-readiness learning, practical scenarios, training and support with essential employment checks.

Aged careHealthcareChildcare

Leadership from design to closure

I designed the complete program and its resources, established delivery and monitoring systems, coordinated stakeholders and supported the team throughout implementation. I managed milestone reporting and closed the project by sharing final outcomes and success stories with the Department of Home Affairs.

02 / My contribution

Six dimensions of project leadership.

01

Program design

Created the 11-week course outline and learning resources, integrating guest speakers, practical activities and role-plays to prepare participants for workplace scenarios.

02

Planning & delivery

Developed the Tuesday–Thursday delivery schedule, 9:00 am–2:45 pm, with a 15-minute morning tea break and 30-minute lunch break each day.

03

Governance & reporting

Monitored KPIs and supporting evidence. Delivered three stages of milestone reporting at approximately four-week intervals, including final project reporting.

04

Employment preparation

Coordinated accredited First Aid and CPR training, food handling training, Service Victoria account creation and Working with Children Check application support, and National Police Check submissions.

05

Stakeholder engagement

Involved external stakeholders including Victoria Police, Brimbank City Council and Melton City Council, and arranged guest speakers to enrich program delivery.

06

Team & financial controls

Created a SharePoint-connected dashboard with budget, expenditure, receipts, milestones and a colour-coded task tracker. Trained the team to use the tracker consistently.
